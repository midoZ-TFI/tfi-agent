For nonprofit CEOs and Executive Directors:

Chronic disease disproportionately affects low-income populations. Yet most health interventions are designed for people who can already afford to be healthy.

At The Fitness Initiative, we focus on the gap. Our grant-funded programs serve individuals in Monroe County living with chronic diseases who cannot afford the resources they need. And we do it through evidence-based, community-designed programming that produces measurable outcomes.

This model works. It attracts grants. And it changes lives.

I am always open to sharing our approach with nonprofit leaders who want to strengthen their impact in underserved communities.

Let us connect.
